    Four defensible outcomes

    The safest decision is not always “act.”

    The firewall can approve a bounded proposal, decline it, request more evidence, or place the decision with a human authority.

    01○

    No action

    Evidence supports benign activity and risk remains below the policy threshold.

    02?

    Investigate

    Evidence is missing, stale, conflicting, low-integrity, or otherwise not decision-grade.

    03✓

    Contain reversibly

    Only allow-listed, bounded actions can pass the deterministic control boundary.

    04↑

    Escalate to human

    Critical assets, break-glass identities, or consequential actions remain with accountable authority.

    Evidence boundary

    What this does—and does not—establish.

    The current site reports project-controlled synthetic evidence. It does not turn a proof of mechanics into a claim of operational effectiveness.

    ✓ What is observed

    • The named fixed synthetic scenarios matched their project-authored expectations.
    • Read-only replay produced no authorization, broker, or operational effect.
    • Named malformed and altered inputs were quarantined or blocked as designed.
    • Evidence is bound to exact versions, denominators, and immutable artifacts.
